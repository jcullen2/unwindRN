# Content bank — v1 (Gate 6)

The launch content engine, banked BEFORE launch so launch week makes nothing.
Channel thesis: nurse short-form (TikTok + IG Reels) is the single channel that
matters at our size; r/nursing and nurse group chats are the beta funnel.
The face is John's and/or Maria's call (D-open) — every script below works
with John alone as "the founder who built it for his fiancée."

## Rules (non-negotiable, from CLAUDE.md + the brand)
- Plain human sentences. No exclamation points. No corporate wellness voice.
- John speaks as the fiancé/founder, NEVER as a nurse or for nurses.
- No patients, ever: no patient stories, imagery, ages+diagnoses, "a kid I
  saw" content. Maria's specifics only with her explicit sign-off, per piece.
- Never guilt, never streaks, never "self-care" lecturing, never therapy
  claims. The app is a record, not a treatment.
- App footage = real captures (sim at 21:00 per the screenshot law), no mockups.
- CTA is the waitlist until launch day; then "on the App Store."

## Three repeatable formats
- **F1 · The number** — an app capture carries the video; one line of VO or
  on-screen text about what the number holds. Cheap, endlessly repeatable.
- **F2 · The gap** — John talking-head, 20–40s, the founder story told in
  pieces. The differentiated asset no competitor can copy.
- **F3 · The promise** — trust content: show a privacy promise being TRUE
  (screen recording of deletion, the scrubber catching a name, airplane-mode
  save). Rare on the platform; built for stitches and saves.

## Scripts 1–12

**01 · "741 shifts" (F1)** — HOOK on screen: "My fiancée has worked 741
shifts. I can show you every one." VISUAL: career grid drawing in, slow.
VO: "One square per shift. The mint ones are nights. She carried every single
one of these home." CTA: "unwindRN — her career, counted. Link for early
access." CAPTION: One square, one shift. 741 and counting.

**02 · "Nobody counts it for them" (F1)** — HOOK: "Nurses chart everything,
all shift. Nobody charts it for them." VISUAL: Home totals → logbook month
filling. VO: "Twelve-hour shifts, charted to the minute — and at the end of
the year her own career is a guess. So we stopped guessing." CTA: waitlist.

**03 · "Five years of nights" (F1)** — HOOK: "What five years of rotating
nights actually looks like." VISUAL: the mint squares isolating out of the
grid. VO: "296 nights. About a year of her life, awake while everyone slept."
CAPTION: The mint ones are nights.

**04 · "The code" (F2)** — HOOK: "She came home after running a code. I
didn't know what to say." John, talking head, quiet. "I'm not a nurse. I
never know what to say. That's kind of why I built this — something that
listens like a coworker who was there, and keeps the record of what she
carried. It's called unwindRN." NO app footage; just him. CTA soft: link in
bio.

**05 · "Twenty seconds" (F1)** — HOOK: "The whole ask is twenty seconds."
VISUAL: real-time tap-through — hours, load, tags, Log it. Timer burned in
the corner. VO: none; let the timer do it. CAPTION: Clock out. Tap. Done.

**06 · "Your voice never leaves your phone" (F3)** — HOOK: "The audio never
leaves her phone. Not marketing — architecture." VISUAL: debrief screen,
airplane-mode toggle ON, teleprompter still transcribing live. VO: "Speech
becomes text on the phone itself. There is no server hearing this." CTA:
"How it's built — plain-English privacy policy, link in bio."

**07 · "No manager dashboard" (F3)** — HOOK: "Your hospital can't see this.
There's nothing for them to buy." VO: "No employer version. No unit reports.
It answers to the nurse holding the phone, and no one else." VISUAL: the
site's trust band, slow scroll. CAPTION: On purpose.

**08 · "The word we banned" (F2)** — HOOK: "This app will never say
'streak.'" John: "Nurses get enough guilt from machines. Miss a week, miss a
month — your record just waits. 'Save without talking' is always one tap.
That's the whole philosophy." CAPTION: No streaks. No guilt. Ever.

**09 · "Just save it" (F1)** — HOOK: "The button for the nights you don't
want to talk about." VISUAL: debrief taps → the quiet "Just save it" link →
saved. VO: "Some shifts don't need words. It still counts." CAPTION: It
still counts.

**10 · "2,960 miles" (F1)** — HOOK: "~2,960 miles on her feet." VISUAL: the
miles slide. VO: "Farther than walking Los Angeles to New York. In Danskos."
CAPTION: Los Angeles to New York, in Danskos.

**11 · "Her orientee's first solo" (F1)** — HOOK: "Her orientee ran her
first admission solo today." VISUAL: the record screen with a precepting
win + the Precepting tag igniting. VO: "The wins nurses shrug off are the
ones the record keeps." CAPTION: Precepting counts too.

**12 · "Delete means delete" (F3)** — HOOK: "Watch me delete an account for
real." VISUAL: screen recording — Delete account → type DELETE → gone →
fresh sign-in shows nothing. VO: "Every record, the account itself,
immediately. We test this before every release." CAPTION: Your record is
yours — including the right to burn it.
