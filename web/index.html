The part that matters

Built to be on your side.

On-device

Your voice never leaves your phone.

Speech becomes text on the phone itself, using Apple's on-device recognition. The audio is never sent anywhere and never stored — that's how it's built, not a promise in fine print.

No employer version

Your hospital never sees this.

No manager dashboard, no unit reports, no data sold upstream. There is nothing for an employer to buy. unwindRN answers to the nurse holding the phone, and no one else.

One tap, in the app

Delete means delete.

Deleting your account removes every record and the account itself, immediately. We tested it end to end before writing this sentence — zero rows left.

The whole story is in the privacy policy — written to be read, not scrolled past.

Why this exists

Built at a kitchen table in Detroit.

My fiancée is a pediatric oncology nurse. She works twelve-hour shifts. Some nights she comes home having run a code on a kid; some nights a bell got rung and the whole floor cheered. I could never hold all of it for her — so I built the thing that could.

unwindRN keeps her shifts, her wins, the weight, and the career they add up to. It was built for one nurse, on her side. It stays that way for every nurse who uses it.

— John, founder · unwindRN LLC

Fair questions

What nurses ask us.

How long does logging actually take?

About twenty seconds if you just tap through. There's always a one-tap "just save it" — no guilt, no follow-up questions.

Do I have to talk to the AI thing?

No. Plenty of people will only ever tap and go. The debrief is there for the nights you walk out carrying something — use it then, skip it otherwise.

Can my hospital see my log?

No. There's no employer version, no manager dashboard, and we don't sell data. Your log is yours — delete it any time, right in the app. Export is on the way.

What if I mention a patient?

The app is built to keep patient details out. It never asks about patients, and anything identifying — names, rooms, record numbers — gets stripped before an entry is saved. Your log is about your night, not their chart.

What happens to my voice?

Speech is turned into text right on your phone — the audio never leaves it. The conversation isn't stored either; only the entry you approve gets saved. The whole thing is in the privacy policy in plain English.

Is it free? Is there Android?

Free right now, while it's in beta. iPhone only for now — Android isn't announced. If that changes, the list hears first.
