Fair questions

What a nurse needs to know.

Can I install this tonight?

No. There is no app in the store tonight. Leave an email. We write when there is a build you can install.

Can my hospital see this?

No. There is no manager seat, no hospital login, and no connection to UKG, Epic, or Workday. Nothing entered here is sent to a manager, a unit, or a health system. There is no employer API.

What do you actually log?

The shift you just worked: hours, nights, charge, precepting. Professional evidence. Not gratitude, not sleep, not a second chart. Do not put patient names, rooms, or record numbers in it.

Do I have to talk?

No. Voice is optional after a hard night. When there is a build, most nights you tap and save. Speech is turned into text on the phone. Audio is never uploaded. The conversation is not stored. Only the entry you approve is saved.

If you are in crisis, call or text 988. That is the US Suicide and Crisis Lifeline. It is not gated.

Who operates this?

unwindRN LLC, a Michigan company. For nurses. Not hospitals. We do not contact your employer. There is no manager dashboard.

What happens to my email?

Product mail only. Not sold. Not shared with any employer. To remove the address, write support@unwindrn.com.

Is there an app I can open?

No. There is no store listing tonight. This page is a waitlist. We write when there is a build.

Can I delete this?

The waitlist address: write support@unwindrn.com. When an account exists, delete will exist; the privacy policy is the rule. We do not promise a file you can take with you until export ships.
